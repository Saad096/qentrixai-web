# Image brief, route by route

Generated by `node scripts/image-brief.mjs`. Do not hand-edit: add a service,
industry, case study, product or article and re-run it.

## What each image is for

Every page carries a card in the **top right of its hero**, the slot the
looping video occupies on the home page. It is the first thing beside the
headline, so it should show the *subject of the page*, not the company.

**House style, so the set holds together:**

- **Line art or technical drawing**, not photography and not 3D renders, except
  for the industry pages where a real photograph of the setting works better.
- **Landscape 4:3** for hero cards (the home video is 3:4 because it sits beside
  a three-line headline; everything else is wider).
- **A subject, not a metaphor.** A drone over a crop row beats a glowing brain.
- **No invented UI.** If it looks like a screenshot of a product, it will be read
  as one. Diagrams and drawings are safe; fake dashboards are not.
- **No vendor logos**, no recognisable faces, no client names.
- Works on a warm graphite `#262621` ground and a stone `#E3E1D8` one. A drawing
  on a transparent or near-white background needs a dark twin, or it glares.

Drop files into `public/images/<section>/<slug>.webp` and tell me; I will wire
them up and generate the dark variants where they are needed.

## Priority, if you are making these by hand

1. **Industry pages without a photograph** — the widest visible gap, and the
   pages a buyer lands on from search.
2. **The ten top-level routes** — most traffic, and they set the house style.
3. **Capability pages**, starting with the four that open most engagements:
   generative-ai, agentic-ai, rag-enterprise-search, voice-ai.
4. Articles and product covers last; both already render something reasonable.
